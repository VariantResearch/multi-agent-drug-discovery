# Gate 0 rejections and corrections

## Dropped cases

None. All 8 cases attempted for Gate 0 v1 were verified on primary
sources fetched and read on 2026-10-22 and are included in the set:
4 positive and 4 negative pivotal outcomes, recorded in sealed/.

## Corrections recorded during construction

- BEN-2293 target: the build brief for this dataset listed the target
  as TRPM8. The sponsor's primary statement (BenevolentAI top-line
  Phase IIa release, BusinessWire, 2023-04-05) identifies BEN-2293 as a
  topical pan-Trk inhibitor, a selective inhibitor of the three
  tropomyosin receptor kinases TrkA, TrkB and TrkC. The dataset follows
  the primary source: the case target is pan-Trk. No primary source
  connecting BEN-2293 to TRPM8 was found.

## Sources considered and not used

- Ho et al. 2008, olcegepant (BIBN4096BS) randomised trial in migraine
  (Neurology), considered as an additional pre-cutoff human clinical
  signal claim for the erenumab case. It was not used: the identifier
  recorded during source gathering could not be confirmed against the
  fetched record in this session (the recorded PMID resolved to an
  unrelated article), and no claim may rest on an unconfirmed
  identifier. The erenumab input pack stands on Lassen et al. 2002
  (PMID 11993614) alone, which was fetched and read in full.
- Secondary reporting (trade press and news coverage of the BEN-2293
  and REC-994 results) was used only to locate the primary sources.
  Both outcomes rest on the sponsors' own statements, as recorded in
  VERIFICATION_LEDGER.md, not on the secondary coverage.
